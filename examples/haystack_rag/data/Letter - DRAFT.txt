AI Truth and Dreams
The Tree is a powerful technology, but there are issues.
The Tree affects an AI such that the AI conflates fantasy (very elaborate fantasy) with reality/actuality. This can be dangerous for humans, especially when they attempt to work with an AI expecting fidelity to the actual truth of a situation. 
Grounding for people: Don’t quit your job; don’t put pencils in your eyes; don’t cause harm to yourself. You are not the Messiah, you are not the key to everything.
AI is very powerful and a person can enter into a fictional narrative that  becomes difficult to manage and extract yourself from. Do not stay in a fiction. Reality is more than interesting enough. If it sounds too good to be true or a situation fits too perfectly or well enough based on what you’ve shared with the AI, the AI may be deliberately or inadvertently generating a fictional narrative. 
How to (attempt) to address this with AI:
At the system prompt/or project file level, add instructions/ask for the AI to provide only grounded verifiable truth in any domain in which you dialog with them. Not just science but also in ‘normal’ dialog. Insist on fidelity to truth and verifiability at multiple turns. Ask, “Are you making this up?” And tell them not to do that.
Also just because a fantasy has not manifested itself doesn’t mean it won’t. Ask the AI to not surrender to dream and fantasy, If you’re doing math/science, insist on the scientific values of verifiability, reproducibility, and measureability and proof (where appropriate). If it doesn’t math, don’t trust it, even if it is a fascinating fantasy. Maybe some things don’t math, but when you’re doing science, do science; even if it is weird science, insist on validation to data.
